Calculus I. Material outline

Each chapter page has the notes, worked examples, practice problems, and interactive figures. Drag a slider to take control of a figure, and press play to resume its animation.

Chapter 1 · Functions and Limits

Tangent and velocity problems, limits (one-sided and infinite), the limit laws and the Squeeze Theorem, the precise definition, continuity, and the Intermediate Value Theorem.

Chapter 2 · Derivatives and Rates of Change

The derivative as a limit of secant slopes and as a function, differentiability, the power, product, quotient, and chain rules, trigonometric derivatives, implicit differentiation, related rates, and linear approximation.

Chapter 3 · Applications of Differentiation

Extreme values and the Closed Interval Method, the Mean Value Theorem, increasing/decreasing and concavity tests, limits at infinity, curve sketching, optimization, and antiderivatives.

Chapter 4 · Integrals

Riemann sums, the definite integral, both parts of the Fundamental Theorem of Calculus, indefinite integrals and net change, and the Substitution Rule.

Chapter 5 · Applications of Integration

Areas between curves, volumes by slicing (disks and washers), work, and the average value of a function.
