Calculus II. Material outline

pick a chapter. each page has the full notes, with interactive figures you can drag and slide.

Chapter 6 · Inverse Functions

Inverse functions and the derivative of an inverse, exponentials and logarithms, logarithmic differentiation, exponential growth and decay, inverse trig and hyperbolic functions, and L'Hôpital's rule.

Chapter 7 · Techniques of Integration

Integration by parts, trig integrals, trig substitution, partial fractions, a strategy for choosing a method, approximate integration (left, right, midpoint, trapezoid, Simpson), and improper integrals.

Chapter 9 · Differential Equations

Modeling with differential equations, direction fields and Euler's method, separable equations and mixing problems, and the logistic equation.

Chapter 11 · Sequences and Series

Sequences, series and partial sums, the integral, comparison, alternating, ratio and root tests, power series and their intervals of convergence, and Taylor series with Taylor's inequality.

Appendix H · Complex Numbers

Arithmetic of $a + bi$, modulus and polar form, multiplication as rotate and scale, De Moivre's theorem and $n$th roots, and Euler's formula $e^{i\theta} = \cos\theta + i\sin\theta$.
