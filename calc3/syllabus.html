Course topics

Multivariable calculus, vector-valued functions, volume, and line and surface integrals.

  • Ch 10. parametric curves, calculus with parametrics (slope, arc length, area), polar coordinates, calculus in polar coordinates.
  • Ch 12. three-dimensional coordinates, vectors, dot and cross products, equations of lines and planes, quadric surfaces.
  • Ch 13. vector functions and space curves, derivatives and integrals, arc length and curvature, motion in space.
  • Ch 14. functions of several variables, limits and continuity, partial derivatives, tangent planes, chain rule, directional derivatives and the gradient, maxima/minima, Lagrange multipliers.
  • Ch 15. double integrals over rectangles and general regions, polar coordinates, applications, triple integrals, cylindrical and spherical coordinates.
  • Ch 16. vector fields, line integrals (scalar, work, flux), the Fundamental Theorem for Line Integrals, Green’s Theorem, curl and divergence.

Life happens

Homework and quizzes are due on their due date. Late homework isn't accepted and there are no makeup quizzes. To cover the unexpected, your 2 lowest quiz scores and 2 lowest homework scores are dropped.

Makeup exams

Makeup exams will not be given except in documented emergencies. If you need to reschedule due to a documented emergency (e.g., surgery), let your instructor know as soon as possible. If you miss an exam due to sickness, contact your instructor immediately. Do not schedule personal travel during exams. Non-NCAA sporting events are not university-authorized emergencies.

Homework

A main goal of the course is to develop your mathematical writing skills, clearly showing all steps using correct algebra and notation. Homework is graded on clarity and correctness of presentation. Submit neat, legible solutions with problems in order. Solutions that are hard to find or read receive zero credit. The same standards apply to exams.

You are welcome to work together on homework. However, the writeup you hand in must be your own work, in your own words. After getting questions answered, you need to be able to do all problems on your own.
