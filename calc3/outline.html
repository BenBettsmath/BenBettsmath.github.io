Calculus III. Material outline

pick a chapter. each page has the full notes, the original outline figures from the latex source, and rotatable 3D versions.

Chapter 10 · Parametric Curves & Polar Coordinates

Curves in parametric and polar form: slope, arc length, and area for each.

Chapter 12 · Vectors and the Geometry of Space

3D geometry: vectors, dot and cross products, lines and planes, and the six quadric surfaces classified by their traces.

Chapter 13 · Vector Functions and Space Curves

Vector-valued functions $\vec r(t)$. Tangents, arc length, curvature, motion, projectiles, and taylor approximation of curves.

Chapter 14 · Partial Derivatives & Gradients

Functions of several variables, partial derivatives, gradient and directional derivative, tangent planes, the chain rule, optimisation (Hessian and Lagrange), and Taylor approximation.

Chapter 15 · Multiple Integrals

Double and triple integrals in polar, cylindrical, and spherical coordinates. volume elements, plus mass, centre of mass, and probability density.

Chapter 16 · Vector Calculus

Vector fields, conservative fields, line integrals (work and fence area), the Fundamental Theorem for Line Integrals, Green's Theorem, curl and divergence.
