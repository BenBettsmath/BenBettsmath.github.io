Common Problems

  1. Show that no group of a given order $N$ is simple.
  2. Compute the Galois group of a given polynomial over $\mathbb{Q}$.
  3. Determine whether $\mathbb{Z}[\sqrt{d}]$ is a UFD, PID, or Euclidean domain.
  4. Show that the automorphism group of an algebraic closure of a finite field is uncountable.
  5. Determine whether a given group is solvable.
  6. Classify the $m \times m$ matrices over $\mathbb{F}_q$ satisfying $A^k = I$.
  7. Determine which abelian groups of order at most $M$ are projective (or injective) as $\mathbb{Z}/n\mathbb{Z}$-modules.
  8. Show that a nontrivial $p$-group has nontrivial center, and that a group of order $p^2$ is abelian.
  9. Exhibit a non-split short exact sequence of modules.
  10. Compute the splitting field of a polynomial over $\mathbb{Q}$ and its degree.
  11. Compute a tensor product of abelian groups over $\mathbb{Z}$.
  12. Describe the maximal ideals of $C[0,1]$.
  13. Show that $\mathbb{Q}$ is injective but not projective as a $\mathbb{Z}$-module.
  14. Show that the nilradical of a commutative ring equals the intersection of its prime ideals.
  15. Describe all intermediate fields of a Galois extension of degree $n$.
  16. If $[\mathbb{Q}(\alpha):\mathbb{Q}] = m$, $[\mathbb{Q}(\beta):\mathbb{Q}] = n$, $\gcd(m,n) = 1$, show $[\mathbb{Q}(\alpha,\beta):\mathbb{Q}] = mn$.
  17. Show that $\mathbb{C}[x^2, x^3]$ is not a UFD.
  18. Show that $AB - BA \neq I$ for $n \times n$ matrices over a field of characteristic not dividing $n$.
  19. Show that the nilpotency index of an $n \times n$ nilpotent matrix is at most $n$.
  20. If $|\mathrm{Aut}(G)| = 1$, show $G$ is abelian and every element has order at most $2$.
  21. Show that a ring with $x^2 = x$ (or $x^3 = x$) for all $x$ is commutative.
  22. Show $K[[x]]$ is local with maximal ideal $(x)$ and is a PID.
  23. Show that $V \otimes V \cong S^2 V \oplus \Lambda^2 V$ when $\mathrm{char}(k) \neq 2$.
  24. Show that the free group of rank $2$ contains the free group of rank $3$.
  25. Compute $S^{-1}R$ for a specific multiplicative set $S$.
  26. Describe $\mathbb{Q}(\zeta_n)$ and its subfields.
  27. Prove an isomorphism statement via a diagram chase or the short five lemma.
  28. Construct a Galois extension with a prescribed finite Galois group.
  29. Describe the Sylow $p$-subgroups of $S_n$.
  30. Determine the reducibility of $1 + t + t^2 + \cdots + t^n = (t^{n+1} - 1)/(t - 1)$ in $\mathbb{Q}[t]$.
