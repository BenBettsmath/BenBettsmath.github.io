Common Problems

  1. Show that no group of a given order $N$ is simple. Sylow counting, or the action on the set of Sylow subgroups.
  2. Compute the Galois group of a given polynomial over $\mathbb{Q}$. Splitting field, its degree, and the action on the roots.
  3. Determine whether $\mathbb{Z}[\sqrt{d}]$ is a UFD, PID, or Euclidean domain. Norm arguments, or two distinct factorizations of one element.
  4. Show that the automorphism group of an algebraic closure of a finite field is uncountable. Compatible choices of Frobenius powers along the chain $\mathbb{F}_{p^{2^k}}$, one for every binary sequence.
  5. Determine whether a given group is solvable. Burnside for $|G| = p^a q^b$, Feit-Thompson for odd order, or the derived series.
  6. Classify the $m \times m$ matrices over $\mathbb{F}_q$ satisfying $A^k = I$. Factor $x^k - 1$ over $\mathbb{F}_q$ and enumerate invariant factors.
  7. Determine which abelian groups of order at most $M$ are projective (or injective) as $\mathbb{Z}/n\mathbb{Z}$-modules. Projective equals free. Injectivity is checked one prime at a time.
  8. $p$-group facts. Order $p^2$ is abelian, the center of a nontrivial $p$-group is nontrivial, and non-abelian order $p^3$ has $Z(G) = [G,G] \cong \mathbb{Z}/p\mathbb{Z}$.
  9. Exhibit a non-split short exact sequence of modules. $0 \to \mathbb{Z} \xrightarrow{\times 2} \mathbb{Z} \to \mathbb{Z}/2\mathbb{Z} \to 0$.
  10. Compute the splitting field of a polynomial over $\mathbb{Q}$ and its degree. Factor in stages and apply the tower law.
  11. Compute a tensor product of abelian groups over $\mathbb{Z}$. $\mathbb{Z}/m \otimes_\mathbb{Z} \mathbb{Z}/n \cong \mathbb{Z}/\gcd(m,n)$.
  12. Describe the maximal ideals of $C[0,1]$. Exactly the $\mathfrak{m}_t = \{f : f(t) = 0\}$, by compactness.
  13. Show that $\mathbb{Q}$ is injective but not projective as a $\mathbb{Z}$-module. Divisible, and not free.
  14. Show that the nilradical of a commutative ring equals the intersection of its prime ideals. For $r$ not nilpotent, Zorn gives a prime avoiding the powers of $r$.
  15. Describe all intermediate fields of a Galois extension of degree $n$. Subgroups of the Galois group, via the fundamental theorem.
  16. If $[\mathbb{Q}(\alpha):\mathbb{Q}] = m$, $[\mathbb{Q}(\beta):\mathbb{Q}] = n$, $\gcd(m,n) = 1$, show $[\mathbb{Q}(\alpha,\beta):\mathbb{Q}] = mn$. Tower law in both directions.
  17. Show that $\mathbb{C}[x^2, x^3]$ is not a UFD. Not integrally closed, since $x$ is integral over it but outside it.
  18. Show that $AB - BA \neq I$ for $n \times n$ matrices over a field of characteristic not dividing $n$. Take the trace.
  19. Show that the nilpotency index of an $n \times n$ nilpotent matrix is at most $n$. Cayley-Hamilton with $\chi_A(x) = x^n$.
  20. If $|\mathrm{Aut}(G)| = 1$, show $G$ is abelian and every element has order at most $2$. Trivial inner automorphisms, then $g \mapsto g^{-1}$.
  21. Rings satisfying $x^n = x$. For $n = 2$ expand $(x + y)^2$. For $n = 3$ set $x = 2$.
  22. Show $K[[x]]$ is local with maximal ideal $(x)$ and is a PID. Units are the series with nonzero constant term, and every nonzero ideal is $(x^k)$.
  23. Show that $V \otimes V \cong S^2 V \oplus \Lambda^2 V$ when $\mathrm{char}(k) \neq 2$. Split $v \otimes w$ into symmetric and antisymmetric parts.
  24. Show that the free group of rank $2$ contains the free group of rank $3$. Nielsen-Schreier and the index formula, rank $k(n - 1) + 1$.
  25. Compute $S^{-1}R$ for a specific multiplicative set $S$. Primes of $S^{-1}R$ are the primes of $R$ disjoint from $S$.
  26. Describe $\mathbb{Q}(\zeta_n)$ and its subfields. $\mathrm{Gal}(\mathbb{Q}(\zeta_n)/\mathbb{Q}) \cong (\mathbb{Z}/n\mathbb{Z})^\times$ of order $\varphi(n)$.
  27. Prove an isomorphism statement via a diagram chase or the short five lemma.
  28. Construct a Galois extension with a prescribed finite Galois group. Abelian groups arise as subfields of $\mathbb{Q}(\zeta_n)$.
  29. Describe the Sylow $p$-subgroups of $S_n$. For $n < p^2$, a product of $\lfloor n/p \rfloor$ disjoint $p$-cycles, elementary abelian.
  30. Determine the reducibility of $1 + t + t^2 + \cdots + t^n = (t^{n+1} - 1)/(t - 1)$ in $\mathbb{Q}[t]$. Cyclotomic factorization. Irreducible iff $n + 1$ is prime.
