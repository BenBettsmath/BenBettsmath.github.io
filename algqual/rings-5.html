Qual problems · Ring Theory

  1. (Aug 2024 #3) Let $R$ satisfy $x^3 = x$ for all $x \in R$. (a) Prove $\mathrm{char}(R) \mid 6$. (b) Give two examples of different characteristic. (c) If $\mathrm{char}(R) = 2$, show $R$ is Boolean and hence commutative.
  2. (Jan 2024 #2) Let $R$ be a Boolean ring ($x^2 = x$ for all $x$). Prove $R$ is commutative.
  3. (Aug 2024 #5) Let $I = (x^2, xy)$ in $\mathbb{Q}[x, y]$. Show $I$ is not prime. Find two prime ideals containing $I$. Are either of them maximal?
  4. (Jan 2024 #4) Let $R$ be commutative, $f \in R$ nonzero, $S = \{f^n : n \geq 0\}$. Prove $S^{-1}R \cong R[x]/(fx - 1)$.
  5. (Aug 2023 #5) Let $R$ be commutative and $x, y \in R$ nilpotent. Prove that $x + y$ is nilpotent.
  6. (Jan 2021 #4) Prove that in a commutative ring with $1$, the intersection of all prime ideals equals the set of nilpotent elements.
  7. (Aug 2022 #10) Prove that $K[[x]]$ is local and principal.
  8. (Aug 2023 #4) Find all maximal ideals in $\mathbb{Z}/100\mathbb{Z}$. If $S \subseteq \mathbb{Z}$ is the multiplicative set of powers of $100$, find all maximal ideals in $S^{-1}\mathbb{Z}$.
  9. (Jan 2021 #5) Let $R$ be the ring of continuous functions $[a, b] \to \mathbb{R}$ and $M$ a maximal ideal. Prove there is $c \in [a, b]$ with $M = \{f : f(c) = 0\}$.
  10. (Aug 2020 #1) Show that $\mathbb{Z}[\sqrt{-13}]$ is not a Euclidean domain.
  11. (Jan 2024 #5) Prove that the ring $\mathbb{Z}[\sqrt{10}]$ is not a UFD.
  12. (Aug 2024 #4) Prove that the ring $\mathbb{C}[x^3, x^4]$ is not a UFD.
  13. (Aug 2022 #4) Determine whether $\mathbb{Z}[\sqrt{-11}]$ is a UFD.
