Qual problems · Module Theory

  1. (Aug 2024 #6) Let $R = \mathbb{Z}/56\mathbb{Z}$. Determine all abelian groups of order at most $100$ that are injective $R$-modules.
  2. (Jan 2024 #6) Let $R = \mathbb{Z}/100\mathbb{Z}$. Determine all abelian groups of order at most $125$ that are projective $R$-modules.
  3. (Aug 2023 #7) Let $R = \mathbb{Z}/120\mathbb{Z}$. Determine all abelian groups of order at most $200$ that are injective $R$-modules.
  4. (Jan 2022 #6) Let $R = \mathbb{Z}/12\mathbb{Z}$. Determine up to isomorphism all quotients of $R^2$ that are projective $R$-modules.
  5. (Jan 2022 #4) Let $R$ be commutative and $M$ an $R$-module. (a) Show $\mathrm{ann}_R(M) = \{r : rm = 0 \text{ for all } m\}$ is an ideal. (b) For $R = k[x^2, xy, y^2]$ and $M = R/(x^2, xy) \oplus R/(xy, y^2)$, determine $\mathrm{ann}_R(M)$.
  6. (Aug 2022 #5) Let $\mathrm{char}\,F \neq 2$ and $V$ a f.g. $F$-vector space. Prove $V \otimes_F V \cong S^2(V) \oplus \Lambda^2(V)$.
  7. (Jan 2024 #7) Prove: (a) $\mathbb{Z}/3 \otimes_\mathbb{Z} \mathbb{Z}/2 = 0$. (b) $\mathbb{Z}/4 \otimes_\mathbb{Z} \mathbb{Z}/2 \cong \mathbb{Z}/2$.
  8. (Jan 2024 #3) Give an example of a non-split exact sequence of $\mathbb{C}[x]$-modules and justify.
  9. (Aug 2021 #9) Prove that if $R$ is an integral domain and $I$ a nonzero proper ideal, then $0 \to I \to R \to R/I \to 0$ does not split.
  10. (Aug 2022 #2) Let $H \leq \mathbb{Z}^4$ be generated by $(2,2,2,2), (2,4,8,0), (8,8,0,4)$. Determine $\mathbb{Z}^4/H$ as a product of cyclic groups.
