Qual problems · Group Theory

  1. (Jan 2024 #1) Show that there is no simple group of order $80$.
  2. (Jan 2022 #1) Show that no group of order $132$ is simple.
  3. (Aug 2021 #1) Show that no group of order $72$ is simple.
  4. (Jan 2020 #1) Let $G$ be a group of order $402$. Prove that $G$ is not simple.
  5. (Aug 2024 #2) Let $G$ be a group of order $3 \cdot 2^n$ for $n \geq 1$. Show that $G$ is solvable.
  6. (Aug 2023 #3) Prove that the group of upper-triangular matrices with real coefficients is solvable.
  7. (Jan 2021 #2) Prove that the group of $2 \times 2$ invertible complex matrices is not solvable.
  8. (Jan 2022 #2) Let $p$ be an odd prime and $G$ a group of order $p^3$. Show that $Z(G) = [G, G]$.
  9. (Jan 2022 #3) Let $G$ have normal subgroups $H$ and $K$. Show that if $G/H$ and $G/K$ are nilpotent, then $G/(H \cap K)$ is nilpotent.
  10. (Aug 2023 #2) Let $G$ satisfy $|\mathrm{Aut}(G)| = 1$. Show that $G$ is abelian and every element of $G$ has order at most $2$.
  11. (Aug 2020 #2) Consider $G = S_{28}$. What is the order of a Sylow $7$-subgroup of $G$? Is it abelian?
  12. (Jan 2021 #1) Let $F_2 = \langle a, b \rangle$ and let $H$ be the subgroup generated by $bab^{-1}, b^2 a b^{-2}, b^3 a b^{-3}$. Prove $H \cong F_3$.
