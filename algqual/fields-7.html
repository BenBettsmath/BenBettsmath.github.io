Qual problems · Field Theory

  1. (Aug 2024 #9) Prove that the Galois group of $x^4 - 3$ over $\mathbb{Q}$ is not abelian.
  2. (Jan 2024 #9) Prove that the Galois group of $x^5 - 2$ over $\mathbb{Q}$ is non-commutative.
  3. (Aug 2022 #8) Determine the Galois group of $x^3 - 3x + 1$ over $\mathbb{Q}$.
  4. (Jan 2022 #8) Determine the splitting field $E$ of $x^3 + 2$ over $\mathbb{Q}$, and the Galois group $\mathrm{Gal}(E/\mathbb{Q})$.
  5. (Aug 2021 #2) Determine the Galois group of $x^4 + 4$ over $\mathbb{Q}$.
  6. (Jan 2020 #8) Compute the Galois group of $x^7 - 10$ over $\mathbb{Q}$.
  7. (Jan 2021 #8) Prove that the Galois group of $x^5 - 7$ over $\mathbb{Q}$ is solvable but not abelian.
  8. (Aug 2024 #10) Suppose $E \subseteq K$ are fields of characteristic $0$ with $[K : E] = 72$ and $K/E$ Galois. Prove there exists an intermediate field $E \subsetneq F \subsetneq K$ with $F$ Galois over $E$.
  9. (Aug 2022 #9) Let $K/F$ be Galois with $[K : F] = 36$. (a) Show there exist intermediate $E_1, E_2$ with $[K : E_1] = 4$, $[K : E_2] = 9$. (b) Show there is an intermediate $E/F$ Galois over $F$.
  10. (Jan 2021 #9) Prove that the automorphism group of the algebraic closure of $\mathbb{Q}$ is uncountable.
