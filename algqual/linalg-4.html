Qual problems · Linear Algebra

  1. (Aug 2024 #8) Suppose $A$ is a $3 \times 3$ matrix over $\mathbb{F}_7$ satisfying $A^9 = I$. Determine the number of conjugacy classes of such $A$.
  2. (Jan 2024 #8) Suppose $A$ is a $4 \times 4$ matrix over $\mathbb{F}_{11}$ satisfying $A^{20} = I$. Determine the number of isomorphism classes of $A$.
  3. (Aug 2023 #8) Suppose $A$ is a $5 \times 5$ matrix over $\mathbb{F}_2$ satisfying $A^7 = I$. Determine all isomorphism classes, listing one matrix from each.
  4. (Jan 2022 #7) Prove that if $V$ is finite-dimensional over a field of characteristic $0$ and $u, v \in \mathrm{End}_K(V)$, then $uv - vu \neq 1$. Give an example showing this can fail in infinite dimension.
  5. (Jan 2020 #10) Let $A$ be a complex $10 \times 10$ matrix with $A^{10000} = 0$. Prove $A^{10} = 0$.
